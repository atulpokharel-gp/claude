# Setting up Microsoft 365 checks

The console finds AI apps that were given access to a client's mail, files and
Teams chats, and can revoke them. It uses one Microsoft Entra app that each
client's admin approves once.

## 1. Register the app (once, in your own Microsoft tenant)

1. Go to the [Microsoft Entra admin center](https://entra.microsoft.com) ›
   App registrations › New registration.
2. Name: `Zinloop Security`. Supported account types: **Accounts in any
   organizational directory (multitenant)**.
3. Redirect URI: platform **Web**, `https://app.zinloop.com/api/m365/callback`
   (your `CONSOLE_URL` plus `/api/m365/callback`).
4. Under **API permissions** › Add › Microsoft Graph › **Application
   permissions**, add:
   - `Application.Read.All` (list apps)
   - `DelegatedPermissionGrant.ReadWrite.All` (read and revoke user and admin approvals)
   - `AppRoleAssignment.ReadWrite.All` (read and revoke app-only permissions)
   - `User.Read.All` (optional: shows the names of the people who approved an
     app on the People page; without it apps are listed without names)
5. Under **Token configuration** › Add groups claim, tick **Directory roles**
   (or set `"groupMembershipClaims": "DirectoryRole"` in the manifest). The
   console uses this to check that the person connecting a tenant is one of
   its admins.
6. Under **Certificates & secrets**, create a client secret. Copy its value.
7. Put the **Application (client) ID** in `M365_CLIENT_ID` and the secret in
   `M365_CLIENT_SECRET`, then restart the console. Secrets expire: set a
   reminder to renew it.
8. Recommended: complete publisher verification so client admins see a
   verified publisher on the consent screen.

## 2. Connect a client

On the client's page, click **Connect Microsoft 365**. A Global Administrator
of the client's tenant approves the permissions, then signs in once more so
the console can confirm they belong to that tenant (the consent answer alone
can be faked). Only then is the tenant linked. The console checks it straight
away and again every day.

## What it reads and changes

- Reads the tenant's app list, delegated permission grants, app-only
  permission assignments, and the names of the people who approved AI apps.
  It doesn't read mail, files or chats.
- Shows only apps that look like AI tools (by name or publisher) and skips
  Microsoft's own apps.
- **Revoke** deletes that app's permission grants in the tenant. The app stays
  listed in Entra and can be approved again if someone needs it.

## Testing with a free tenant

The [Microsoft 365 Developer Program](https://developer.microsoft.com/microsoft-365/dev-program)
may give you a sandbox tenant. Approve an AI app (for example sign in to ChatGPT
or Otter with a sandbox user), connect the tenant, and check it appears.
